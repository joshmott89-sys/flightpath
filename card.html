Live location (optional)

When this phone has internet (an eSIM or Wi-Fi) and the card is open, it can send its location to the Bali Meetup page. That works on iPhone and Android and needs no Google account. The location is scrambled with the family code, so only people with the code can read it.

Backup contact & health

The paper copy has the meeting point, phone numbers, help phrases and papers, in case the phone is lost or flat. Save the card first, then print it or save it as a PDF.

"Copy setup link" lets you fill this in on your own phone and send it to Dad's. Everything except the photo and papers travels inside the link. Only send it in a private message.

Add the Travel Card to the home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open the card from the new home-screen icon and fill in setup there. On iPhone the icon keeps its own copy of the details.
