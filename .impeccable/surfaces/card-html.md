# Surface: Dad's card (card.html)

Scope: offline emergency and information card on Dad's phone. Mode: Operate.
Audience: Dad (80, low tech confidence, no connection in Bali); also airport staff and strangers he shows it to.
Task: know where to wait and who is coming; show someone a phrase that gets him help.
Constraints: fully offline after first open; personal details only in this phone's localStorage; nothing personal in the repo.

## Direction contract
FORM: Phrase tiles (seed:ee56f734d2dbfc7e; roll ran degraded, no challengers)
THESIS: A card Dad reads at a glance and hands to a stranger. It refuses a settings-style list and small-print emergency info.
OWN-WORLD: FlightPath navy (#0b0f19 family) as the header band holding "Wait at" in huge white type; light ground for sun and glare; 2-column grid of large white tiles, each with a drawn line icon and a two-word English label; system sans, heavy weights.
STORY: Dad sees where to wait and his son's face first. If something goes wrong, he taps one tile and shows the screen.
FIRST VIEWPORT: navy band (Wait at + spot), son row (photo, name, number), then six tiles, all on one phone screen.
SIGNATURE: tapping a tile opens a full-screen arrivals-hall pickup sign: Indonesian line huge, English below, the key detail (phone number or hotel address) in giant tabular type.
RISK: too much text per tile; keep labels to two words.
